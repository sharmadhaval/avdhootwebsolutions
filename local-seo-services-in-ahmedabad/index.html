What Is Local SEO?

Local SEO (Search Engine Optimization) is the specialized process of optimizing your business's digital presence to rank prominently in location-specific Google searches and Google Maps results. When prospective clients in Ahmedabad search for services near them (e.g., "best clinic in Satellite" or "real estate agent in SG Highway"), Local SEO ensures your business appears in the top 3 Google Maps Local Pack with phone number, business address, positive reviews, and direction links.
